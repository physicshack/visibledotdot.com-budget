# Visible
## Positioning Paper — May 2026
*Making the invisible visible*

---

## Origin

When I ran my last business, the heading on my website was *making the invisible visible*.

I was building physics demonstration models — objects designed to take forces that everyone had walked past their whole lives and make them suddenly, unmistakably real. Drop a magnet through a copper pipe and it falls in slow motion. Lenz's law was always there. The demonstration makes it visible. Pour rheoscopic fluid into a tank and you see the vortices that exist in every liquid but are never seen. Tilt a compass on two axes and the inclination of the Earth's magnetic field — present everywhere, noticed by almost nobody — becomes something you can hold in your hand.

The principle was always the same. The force exists. The effect is real. You just need the right instrument to reveal it.

I was late diagnosed with ADHD.

And I realised the same principle had been operating in my own life for decades. The capability was always there. The ideas, the energy, the pattern recognition, the drive. But without the right infrastructure, none of it was consistently visible — not to the people around me, and honestly, not always to me.

The kid who was smart but produced no work.

The adult juggling a thousand obligations, too proud to ask for help, watching opportunities disappear into the gap between intention and execution.

The business owner who qualified for £40,000 in R&D tax credits and never claimed them. Not through negligence. Through the invisible cost of executive function failure.

**Visible** exists because capability should not be limited by infrastructure — and because that infrastructure problem is now, for the first time, technically solvable at scale.

## The Problem Nobody Names Correctly

ADHD is consistently framed as an attention disorder. This framing is wrong in a way that matters.

ADHD is primarily an executive function disorder. Attention is a symptom. The underlying deficit is in the cognitive systems that govern initiation, planning, working memory, emotional regulation, and time perception. The result is not that people with ADHD can't pay attention. It is that they cannot reliably deploy their own capabilities on demand.

The limitation is not in intelligence, creativity, ambition, or ability. It is in the infrastructure that connects those capabilities to consistent output.

The consequences are measurable:

- Adults with ADHD earn on average 17% less than neurotypical peers with equivalent qualifications
- ADHD costs the UK economy an estimated £13 billion annually in lost productivity
- The "ADHD tax" — financial penalties from missed deadlines, forgotten renewals, impulsive decisions, and administrative failure — costs individuals £1,000–£3,000 per year
- Business owners with ADHD are disproportionately represented in both high-growth entrepreneurship and business failure — often the same person at different points in time

The system is not built for this. Banking, government, insurance, tax, and business administration all assume consistent executive function. They penalise its absence quietly and expensively. The costs are almost entirely invisible until they become a crisis.

---

## The Insight

If the limitation is infrastructure, not capability, then the solution is infrastructure.

Not therapy. Not medication alone. Not productivity systems that require the same executive function they are designed to replace.

External cognitive scaffolding. Systems that observe, initiate, remind, execute, and surface — so that the person's actual capabilities are freed for the things only they can do.

This is not a new idea in principle. Personal assistants, accountants, lawyers, and estate managers have performed this function for high-net-worth individuals for centuries. The wealthy have always been able to externalise administrative burden.

The insight is that AI makes this deliverable at scale, at a price point that opens the market to everyone.

## Visible — Executive Function as a Service

Visible is a modular system of AI-powered services that scaffold the specific executive function failures most costly to adults with ADHD — particularly business owners and high-capability individuals whose potential consistently exceeds their administrative output.

Each module makes a different invisible thing visible.

---

### Visible Finance
*The financial forces operating on your life, made real*

Most financial apps assume you already understand your position. They present data. They assume you can construct meaning from it.

Visible Finance starts from the opposite assumption. It constructs the meaning for you and presents the conclusion. Not a dashboard — a conversation. Not data — awareness.

The core insight: ADHD makes it almost impossible to hold the full picture of income, commitments, timing, and consequence in working memory simultaneously. The solution is not to present more data. It is to hold that picture on your behalf and surface only what matters, when it matters.

**What it makes visible:**
- The financial pressure building before payday — shown as a rolling balance projection from your last payday, not from today
- The structural gap between income and commitments that explains why money always feels tight even when the numbers look fine
- The specific bills that need a decision this period and the consequence of each option
- The buffer you actually have versus the buffer you feel you have
- The opportunity cost of inaction — the grant, the saving, the switch you qualified for and didn't make

**Proof of concept:** Visible Finance v1.7 is a working beta, real-user validated, with Claude AI analysis, Firebase household sync, rolling balance projection, and ADHD-appropriate two-mode Plan/Pay separation. Built in two days.

---

### Visible Admin
*The obligations accumulating in silence, surfaced before they become crises*

The second most costly failure domain for ADHD adults is correspondence and administrative obligation. Not because the tasks are hard. Because the activation cost of initiating them is prohibitive, and the consequence of not initiating them is invisible until it isn't.

The letter that becomes a fine. The renewal that lapses. The HMRC notice that required a response thirty days ago. The contract clause that triggered automatically because nobody tracked it.

Visible Admin ingests and categorises correspondence, identifies deadlines and required actions, drafts responses for review, and proactively surfaces obligations before they become urgent. The user's role is review and approval. Initiation and tracking are handled by the system.

**What it makes visible:**
- Every open obligation, ranked by consequence and urgency
- The letter sitting in the pile that needs a response this week
- The renewal coming up that you don't know you need to renew
- The relationship that needs a touch because it's been six weeks

---

### Visible Business
*The opportunities you qualified for and never knew existed*

The most asymmetric failure mode of ADHD for business owners is not what they lose — it is what they never gain. Unclaimed funding, missed schemes, unfiled credits. The cost is invisible because you never see the money you didn't claim.

A single R&D tax credit claim can be worth £20,000–£200,000 for an eligible business. SEIS and EIS eligibility windows close. Innovate UK grant rounds open and close with short application windows. Local authority business support schemes go unclaimed because nobody is watching for them.

Visible Business monitors obligations and opportunities, files proactively, and surfaces eligibility as your circumstances change.

**What it makes visible:**
- The R&D tax credit you've been generating and not claiming
- The grant round that opened this week that you're eligible for
- The Companies House filing due in 28 days
- The government scheme your business now qualifies for following last month's contract win

---

### Visible Relationships
*The momentum that disappears in the gap between intention and action*

Business relationships deteriorate when executive function fails at the follow-up stage. A promising conversation goes cold. A proposal is sent and never chased. A client relationship drifts because consistent initiation requires consistent executive function.

Visible Relationships maintains the momentum of professional relationships without requiring the user to hold it in working memory.

**What it makes visible:**
- The conversation that needs a next step
- The relationship that has gone quiet
- The proposal that was sent three weeks ago and never followed up
- The person who said "let's talk after the summer" and it is now October

## The Market

### Consumer

**UK ADHD adults:** approximately 2.5 million, with diagnosis rates rising 400% since 2020 as adult recognition improves. Significant undiagnosed population estimated at 2–4x the diagnosed rate.

**Target segment:** Late-diagnosed adults in professional or entrepreneurial roles. High capability, high frustration, strong motivation for tools that demonstrably work. Disproportionately likely to pay for solutions to a problem they now understand.

**Pricing:** Free until demonstrated value — two months of active engagement with measurable outcome improvement. Then £4.99/month for Visible Finance, £9.99/month for the full Visible suite. No referral fees. No affiliate arrangements. The advice layer must remain completely trusted.

The business model is subscription only. The moment Visible earns money from recommending a product, the trust relationship that makes the advice valuable is compromised. Users with ADHD are already hypervigilant about being taken advantage of. That vigilance is an asset, not an obstacle. We protect it.

### B2B — Employers

Financial stress and executive function failure cost UK employers an estimated £4.8 billion annually in presenteeism and absence. Post-Employment Rights Bill, neurodivergent accommodation is increasingly an expectation rather than a discretionary benefit.

Visible positions as an EAP category product: demonstrable wellbeing benefit, reportable outcomes, ADHD inclusion credential.

At 15% ADHD prevalence in a 500-person organisation, 75 employees are affected. If Visible reduces the ADHD tax by £500/year per person, the ROI on a £15,000 enterprise licence is 2.5x before accounting for absence reduction or presenteeism improvement.

The institution is not buying an app. It is buying outcomes.

### B2B — NHS and IAPT

Financial stress is a primary presenting factor in IAPT referrals. A tool that demonstrably reduces financial anxiety has a credible preventative mental health case.

IAPT cost per patient: approximately £1,200 for a full treatment course. Digital prevention at scale is an active commissioning priority. Evidence-based tools can be recommended through the NHS Apps Library.

Pathway: academic partnership for outcome evidence generation, followed by ICB commissioning conversations.

### B2B — Accountancy Networks

Business-owner clients with ADHD are disproportionately represented in accountancy practices and disproportionately costly to serve. They miss deadlines, lose documents, require chasing, and underutilise services they are paying for.

Visible as a white-label tool for forward-thinking accountancy practices: reduces client management overhead, increases retention of a high-value but high-friction client segment, positions the practice as genuinely ADHD-aware.

## Why Now

Three conditions have converged to make Visible viable in 2026:

**AI capability.** The analysis, drafting, categorisation, and proactive identification tasks that constitute Visible's intelligence layer require language model capability that was not commercially available at this level or price point two years ago.

**ADHD awareness.** Late diagnosis rates have increased dramatically. A large, self-aware adult ADHD population actively seeking solutions now exists. The community is vocal, digitally native, and shares what works.

**API economics.** The cost of AI inference has fallen 90% in 18 months. A consumer-priced product with AI at its core is now economically viable where it wasn't.

## Proof of Concept

Visible Finance v1.7 demonstrates the core thesis in the financial domain:

- **Separation of decision and execution** — Plan mode and Pay mode respect the cognitive distinction between deciding and doing
- **AI analysis layer** — removes the requirement to construct financial understanding independently
- **Proactive surfacing** — identifies pressure points and flags risks without requiring the user to initiate analysis
- **ADHD-appropriate design** — collapsed by default, progressive disclosure, specific and short outputs, no walls of text
- **Household sync** — acknowledges that ADHD affects household dynamics, not just individuals

Built in two days as a single-file progressive web app by a late-diagnosed ADHD founder, while travelling internationally to present an unrelated deep-tech product at a trade show.

That last sentence is not incidental. It is the point.

## The Founder Perspective

Late diagnosis of ADHD reframes a personal history.

What looks from the outside like inconsistency, underperformance relative to apparent capability, or administrative chaos has a structural explanation. The kid who was smart but produced no work. The adult juggling a thousand obligations who was too proud to ask for help. The business owner who kept starting things and watching them stall not from lack of ambition but from lack of infrastructure.

The capability was always there. The visibility wasn't.

Visible is not built from a market research report. It is built from direct knowledge of what the failure feels like and what the relief of the right scaffolding feels like. That is a meaningful founder advantage in a space where most tools are designed by neurotypical builders theorising about a population they don't belong to.

The physics demonstrations taught one thing above everything else: the most powerful effects are the ones that were always present. You just needed the right instrument to make them real.

That is what Visible is.
